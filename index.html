THE HOTEL

Built in 1940, and deliberately never made bigger

El Royale has stood on Ventura Boulevard since 1940, a single-storey Colonial building wrapped around a private courtyard. Fourteen rooms, external walkways, parquet floors, and a front desk that has never closed. The studios grew up around it. We stayed the same size.
